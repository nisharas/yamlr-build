# Design Partners Guide for Yamlr

**Purpose:** This document outlines the strategy for acquiring and working with design partners to validate yamlr before public launch.

---

## What Are Design Partners?

**Design partners are your first 3-5 paying customers who help shape the product in exchange for early access and discounted pricing.**

They're not beta testers. They're not free users. They pay (even if discounted) and give structured feedback.

---

## What Design Partners Do

| Activity | Value to You | Value to Them |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Use yamlr on real clusters** | Real-world validation (not synthetic tests) | Solve their actual YAML problems |
| **Report bugs & edge cases** | Find issues before public launch | Get fixes prioritized |
| **Request features** | Validate PRO tier roadmap | Influence product direction |
| **Provide testimonials** | Social proof for website/decks | Look innovative to their peers |
| **Sign case study** | Marketing asset for sales | Public recognition |
| **Pay money** | Proves market willingness | Early access at discount |

---

## Ideal Design Partner Profile

| Criteria | Why |
|:--|:--|
| **20-100 clusters** | Big enough to have real pain, small enough to move fast |
| **Already on GitOps** (Argo/Flux) | They understand the problem space |
| **Understaffed platform team** (2-5 engineers) | They need automation, not more manual work |
| **Compliance pressure** (SOC2, HIPAA, PCI) | Willing to pay for safety/policy features |
| **Decision maker accessible** | CTO or Platform Lead can sign off quickly |

---

## What You Offer Design Partners

| Offer | Typical Terms |
|:--|:--|
| **Discount** | 50-80% off for first year |
| **Early access** | PRO features before GA |
| **Direct line to you** | Slack/email access to founder |
| **Feature influence** | Their requests prioritized |
| **Onboarding help** | You help them set up |

---

## What You Ask in Return

| Ask | Why It Matters |
|:--|:--|
| **Monthly feedback call** (30 min) | Structured input, not just bug reports |
| **Logo rights** | "Trusted by X" on website |
| **Case study** (post-launch) | Marketing leverage |
| **Reference call** (for investors/prospects) | Warm intro to other buyers |
| **Real payment** (even $100/month) | Validates willingness to pay |

---

## How to Find Design Partners

| Channel | Approach |
|:--|:--|
| **LinkedIn** | Search "Platform Engineer" or "SRE Lead" at 50-200 person startups on K8s |
| **DevOps Slack communities** | Participate genuinely, don't spam; mention problem you solve |
| **Hacker News** | Post a Show HN when ready; serious platform folks lurk there |
| **Twitter/X** | Follow K8s influencers, engage with their content |
| **Friends of friends** | Ask network: "Know anyone running 20+ K8s clusters?" |
| **Conference talks** | Even 10-person meetup talks work for early leads |

---

## Why Design Partners > Free Users

| Free Users | Design Partners |
|:--|:--|
| No skin in the game | Paying = invested |
| Disappear without feedback | Committed to structured feedback |
| "It's free, whatever" | "I paid, I expect value" |
| No testimonial value | "Company X uses yamlr" |
| Can't validate pricing | Proves price point works |

---

## Realistic Timeline

| Week | Goal |
|:--|:--|
| Week 1-2 | Identify 20 target companies |
| Week 3-4 | Cold outreach (LinkedIn/email) to 20 |
| Week 5-6 | Demo to 5-10 interested leads |
| Week 7-8 | Close 2-3 design partners |
| Month 2-3 | Onboard, collect feedback |
| Month 4+ | Case studies, testimonials ready |

---

## Success Metrics

**Goal: 3-5 design partners paying even $200/month each**

This gives you:
- ✅ Real validation ("People pay for this")
- ✅ Real bugs ("This breaks on our 50-namespace cluster")
- ✅ Real testimonials ("Yamlr saved us 4 hours/week")
- ✅ Investor story ("We have paying customers already")

## Outreach Template

### LinkedIn Message (Cold)
```
Hi [Name],

I noticed you're leading platform engineering at [Company]. 

I'm building yamlr—a tool that auto-heals Kubernetes YAML issues 
(syntax, deprecations, cross-resource bugs). We've validated it 
on 15,000+ files with 100% K8s compliance.

Looking for 3-5 design partners to help shape the product. 
Discounted early access + direct influence on the roadmap.

Would you be open to a 15-min chat to see if it's relevant?
```

- "Quick question about [Company]'s K8s setup"
- "Auto-healing for Kubernetes manifests"
